================================================================================
                      GAME CONTROLS
================================================================================

Keyboard:
  ↑ or W         - Rotate piece
  ← or A         - Move left
  → or D         - Move right
  ↓ or S         - Move down
  SPACE          - Quick drop (instant fall)
  P              - Pause/Resume

Mobile:
  Swipe Left     - Move left
  Swipe Right    - Move right
  Swipe Down     - Quick drop
  Swipe Up       - Rotate
  Double Tap     - Rotate
